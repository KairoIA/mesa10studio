Custom software and tools

Tools built around the way you work, not the other way round. We understand your process — what repeats, where time gets lost — and build the solution on what you already use, with artificial intelligence only where it helps.

  • Task and process automation: invoices that file themselves, enquiries that reach whoever should handle them and orders that move into production on their own, with no manual work
  • Voice and WhatsApp assistants that answer for you, book appointments and run your diary
  • Custom management software — ERP and CRM sized for a small business — and in-house tools, built around the way you work
  • Connections between the software and machines you already use (middleware): sales, bookings, payments, stock or production, working as a single system
  • Websites and mobile apps: design, development and upkeep, with our review room, so you can tell us your changes right on the site, easily and clearly
  • Large-scale data search: what would take weeks to find online, verified and organised in a simple tool

24/7 receptionist for a physiotherapy clinic

Health · Custom software · No call left unanswered

In a clinic, the phone rings just when the physiotherapist is with a patient, and every missed call can end up at another clinic. For FisioBoost we built a virtual receptionist that answers around the clock: it speaks naturally, answers with the clinic’s information and, when someone wants an appointment, takes their details and sends them straight to the physiotherapist on WhatsApp, who confirms the time in person. It is ready to grow —taking more details from each patient or booking appointments straight into the diary— but the physiotherapist preferred to keep it simple and stay in control of every appointment.

Print agent for a large copy shop

Custom software · Automatic order printing

Iris Copy is a high-volume copy shop that sells through its website and ships across Spain. Every order had to be printed by hand — download the files, check the options, send them to the printer one by one — while serving the counter. We built them an agent that lives on the shop’s computer: it prepares every paid order with its paper, colour and copies, sends it to the right printer, and the team only has to pack it. Whatever it can’t do reliably, it sets aside and flags for a person. We also designed and delivered a custom app to supervise the agent: from their phone, the shop can see at any moment what it is doing, which orders are queued and how the day is going, and ask it about any order without walking over to the computer.

The tool doesn’t decide.

We use artificial intelligence the way you use a compass: to reach further, more precisely. But the compass doesn’t choose what gets drawn. The tools are within anyone’s reach and get better every month; what you can’t download is judgement, an idea of your own and care for detail.

The roots of the brand

We love Greek culture, and not as decoration: it gives us our lettering, our way of working and even the shade that falls across this website. Three ideas explain it.

Tékhne

For the Greeks, art and craft were a single word, the root of “technology”. That’s why we care as much about how something looks as about how it works.

Read more

The Greeks had a single word for art and for craft: tékhne, knowing how to do something well, with head and hands. It is where the word “technology” comes from. That is why at Mesa 10 we don’t separate the creative from the technical: the newest tool doesn’t replace the craft, it continues it, and it only gives its best in the hands of someone who has mastered it.

Entasis

Greek columns are curved by eye so they look perfect. The tool raises the column; we give it the curve: the detail designed for whoever looks at it.

Read more

Greek columns aren’t straight: they swell slightly towards the middle. A straight-sided column, seen from a distance, seems to thin out in the middle, and the Greeks corrected it by eye, thinking of whoever would look at it. That curve doesn’t come from any rule: it comes from looking. That is why our brand’s lettering is called Éntasis, and why we work the way we do: the tool raises the straight column; we give it the curve that makes it look perfect to whoever sees it.

The olive tree

A new branch grafted onto an old root bears fruit for centuries. That’s how our software and audiovisual work grow: new tools on a timeless craft.

Read more

Athena won the patronage of Athens with a gift: against the spring Poseidon struck from the rock, she planted an olive tree, and the city chose what was useful and lasting over what was spectacular. The olive tree can also be grafted: a new branch on an old root keeps bearing fruit for centuries. That is Mesa 10: two branches, software and audiovisual, each in its own colour, growing from the same trunk, timeless craft, and renewed with today’s tools. That is why its shade falls across this whole website.

What we don’t do

  • Hand over the first thing the machine comes up with. The first result is only the starting point. Every piece goes through several rounds, and through human eyes, before it reaches you.
  • Use templates. Your business isn’t the one next door. We start from your problem, your audience and the way you speak, and every piece is made for you.
  • Hide what is generated. We always say so, clearly: in the piece itself or with the label of the platform where it’s published. Your customers’ trust is worth more than any effect.
  • Tie you to a subscription to keep what is yours. The files and the code are yours from day one. If you ever decide to carry on by yourself, you take everything with you.
